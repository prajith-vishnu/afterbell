AfterBell — built for the Congressional App Challenge. Ray Braswell High School, Denton ISD. Stage 1 demo: all data is local sample data, no accounts and no server yet.

Privacy by design: AfterBell never stores or shows a home address. It stores a neighborhood name only, and shows students as a first name plus a last initial.
